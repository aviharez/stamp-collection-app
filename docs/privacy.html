Privacy policy

Postmory by Goomi Studio · last updated 5 November 2026

In short: your photos and stamps stay on your phone. There is no account, no cloud and no analytics. The app only talks to Google for three things you choose: turning your location into a city name, an optional short ad, and buying Pro.

Photos and stamps

Photos you take or pick, the stamps made from them, album names, captions, notes and scrapbook pages are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We never upload them. They leave your phone only when you share, save, print or back them up yourself.

Camera

The camera is used only while the camera screen is open, to take the photos you shoot. The gallery import uses Android's photo picker, so the app sees only the photos you pick.

Location (optional)

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location when you make a stamp, to print the city and country on its postmark. The coordinates are turned into a place name with Android's built-in geocoder, which sends them to Google's geocoding service; see Google's privacy policy. Only the city, country and approximate coordinates of each stamp are saved, on your device. If you decline, or turn off "Place on postmarks", postmarks show the date only. If a photo you import has its own location, that is used instead.

Ads (optional, never forced)

The app shows no banners, pop-up or start-up ads. A short rewarded ad plays only when you tap a "short ad" button (for example to remove the watermark from one picture). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, app interactions and diagnostics to show and measure ads. Before the first ad, where the law requires it (EEA, UK and some US states), you're asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later under Profile → "Ad privacy choices". Nothing ad-related runs until you tap a "short ad" button. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps.

Purchases

Pro is bought through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; the app only learns whether you own Pro. See the Google Payments privacy notice.

Backups

"Back up to a file" writes one file to a place you choose (your phone, a USB drive, a cloud folder you pick). We never see it. Anyone with the file can open your stamps, so keep it somewhere safe.

Notifications and widgets

The streak reminder and the home-screen widgets work entirely on your device.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in or personal profile.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data. We don't receive it in the first place.

Deleting your data

Delete a stamp or album in the app, or uninstall the app (or clear its storage) to remove everything. Backup files you saved are yours to delete.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (PG).
